About Lytbits

An independent advisory firm headquartered in Singapore, built to provide clear, conflict-free counsel on corporate, restructuring, and valuation matters.

Our Firm

Independent advice, senior attention.

Lytbits Pte. Ltd. was established to give companies, boards, and creditors direct access to independent advisory expertise — without the conflicts that come from also executing the transaction itself.

We work across three core practices — corporate advisory, financial restructuring, and valuation & transaction advisory — bringing the same rigor and independence to every engagement, regardless of size.
